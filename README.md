# OpsDesk

Local-first command center for solo and small service businesses. It finds **leads**, finds **government bids**, tracks **jobs and applications** with an evidence-backed **resume helper**, and keeps every follow-up in one place. It exports spreadsheets you can actually work in, and runs a private dashboard on your own machine.

> **Status: pre-build.** This repo holds the build brief and the reference prototypes.
> - Builders: start with **[BUILD_PROMPT.md](BUILD_PROMPT.md)**, then **[docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md)**.
> - `reference/` contains three working prototypes (LeadFinder, BidFinder, Vetted) to port, then remove.

Principles: you stay in control (nothing runs on its own), nothing is sent or submitted for you, free tiers first, and your data stays on your machine.
