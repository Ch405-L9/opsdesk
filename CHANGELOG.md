# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-30)
- **Leads:** official Google Places Text Search (cache-first, 14-day reuse, quota ledger with an 80% posture), PageSpeed audits, four-axis scoring, pain points, cold-email and call-opener drafts signed from your profile, honest wording when no audit has been run.
- **Bids:** USAspending award fetch for your NAICS codes and state, merge and dedupe, lanes (set-aside, open, subcontract, certification-aware), explained fit score, buyer and incumbent tables, Markdown report.
- **Jobs and resume:** paste-in capture format, private evidence file (no contact data), technology-alignment assessment with gaps and blockers, evidence-cited resume bullets, dated application receipts. Never submits.
- **Workflow:** statuses per area, priorities, follow-ups with date shorthands, append-only notes, done and snooze, a "Today" view in your time zone, and an activity log.
- **Workbook:** Today, Leads, Bids and Jobs sheets; your columns highlighted; dropdowns, ☐/☑ checkboxes, date validation, conditional formatting, hyperlinks, formula-injection guard; three-way-merge re-import; open-file protection; `.prev` backup.
- **Dashboard:** FastAPI + Jinja, loopback only, Host check, CSRF, strict CSP, accessible, dark mode, sortable tables, kanban filters, item pages, quick actions.
- **Tooling:** 101 tests (86% coverage), ruff, mypy, CI on Linux, macOS and Windows, gitleaks.
- Built on the phase 1 skeleton started by Manus (paths, profile, migrations), plus the LeadFinder, BidFinder and Vetted prototypes.
